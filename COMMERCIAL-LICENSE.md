# Commercial licensing

Commercial use requires a separate written agreement signed by Jan Milota and the customer. Jan may decline a request. **This file does not grant a commercial license.** The public [LICENSE](LICENSE) grants noncommercial rights only.

This applies to commercial use of the original tools and to commercial use of forks or modified versions that include Jan's code. Commercial examples include business operations, paid consulting using the calculator, selling copies, embedding it in a commercial product, and monetized hosting when their purpose is primarily directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation.

Every noncommercial use is free of license fees and royalties, without a separate agreement, for individuals and organizations alike. The actual use determines the category. A company can make a noncommercial use, and a nonprofit can make a commercial use. Donations, grants, a salary, or an organizational affiliation alone do not make a use commercial. The public license still requires preservation of the terms and notices when copies are shared.

## Request an agreement

Contact [Jan Milota through the project](https://github.com/Heehaaw/tools/issues) to arrange a private discussion. Describe the tool/version, intended use, customer or organization, distribution or hosting plans, and any changes. Keep confidential business details out of public issues.

Agree the following in writing before commercial use begins:

- The parties, covered software/version, permitted uses, duration and termination terms.
- Whether the grant covers internal use, distribution, hosting, modifications, affiliates or customers. Downstream commercial rights and sublicensing must be expressly agreed.
- A fixed fee, royalty, or another individually negotiated arrangement. If royalties apply, define the rate, revenue base, exclusions, currency, reporting, payment dates and verification rights.
- Rights needed for third-party contributions, plus any support, warranties, liability terms, governing law and dispute process.

No fee, royalty percentage or entitlement to all proceeds is established by this repository. Forking does not transfer ownership of contributors' additions to Jan. A commercial agreement from Jan covers only rights he owns or is authorized to license; a modified version may also need permission from other rights holders.

## Established contract starting point

[PolyForm Commercial](https://github.com/polyformproject/commercial.polyformproject.org) provides standard commercial software agreement templates with an ordering document and terms. It can be a starting point for a specific deal. Its default internal-use, redistribution and service restrictions need review for any commercial fork or hosted service. It is not incorporated into this repository's license, and no uncompleted template or link grants commercial rights. Revenue sharing and downstream rights must be checked against the actual signed agreement.

The public license is a custom adaptation. Have a Czech/EU-qualified lawyer review it and the commercial contract before relying on them for a paid deal or enforcement.
