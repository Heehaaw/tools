How the timing calculation works

For each payment or receipt: amount × [(1 + annual return)months remaining / 12 − 1]. Receipts use negative amounts. Refunds after the comparison end are discounted back to that date.

Enter prices including VAT, except a lease explicitly marked excluding VAT. Results subtract eligible VAT refunds and include disposal VAT when VAT registration is enabled. Income tax, private-use adjustments and fuel or charging are excluded. Partial VAT recovery is a planning approximation.

The balloon loan assumes equal monthly payments with the balloon due alongside the final regular payment. The standard loan has no final outstanding balance. Each loan has its own fixed interest rate. Buying outright pays the shared purchase price at month zero and sells at the comparison end. The years and months controls set the same period for all options. Resale is your estimate, not a guaranteed future value. A kept car is credited as an asset at estimated resale value net of disposal VAT, so it is not treated as cash received.

Opportunity cost carries each cash outflow and inflow to the comparison end at your annual after-tax return. A refund three months after buyout is included and discounted back to the end date. This return is an assumption, not a guaranteed investment. Maintenance assumes steady mileage, using whichever interval comes first. A service due exactly at the end is included. Default seasonal costs cover six evenly spaced changes and storage periods, starting at purchase. VAT on eligible maintenance and tyres is netted against the expense immediately in this comparison, treating next-month deductibility as settled. It creates no outstanding VAT at the end. Lease VAT follows its separate delay, defaulting to deduction with each payment. The buyer pays the VAT-inclusive resale price at the end of the term. Its VAT component is then paid to the tax authority at that same modelled date, leaving the VAT-exclusive proceeds. This tax payment is shown separately and is not a refund. The capital VAT cap applies separately to a outright or loan-funded purchase, or lease buyout.
